Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying and selling cars with Mayo Cars.

Are the new car prices on Mayo Cars official?
Yes. New car prices are the ex-factory prices announced by the manufacturers (checked October 2026). Taxes, registration, freight and insurance are extra, and prices can change — confirm on WhatsApp before booking.
How do I buy a used car listed here?
Tap the WhatsApp button on the car, ask your questions and arrange a viewing. We help with inspection, document checks and a safe transfer.
How much does it cost to sell my car with Mayo Cars?
Listing is free. Send your car details on the Sell Your Car page or on WhatsApp and we will discuss the best way to sell.
Which cities do you cover?
All of Punjab. Our main centres are Lahore, Islamabad and Kasur, and we also deal in Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Gujranwala, Multan, Sialkot, Burewala and other cities.
Can I get a car inspected before buying?
Yes. Book an inspection on the Car Inspection page and we will check the engine, body, paint, suspension and documents.
Can I buy a car on instalments?
Yes, through bank car financing. Use our Car Finance calculator to estimate the monthly instalment, then contact us for help.
Are the used car listings real?
The current used cars are demo listings to show how the website works. Real available cars will be added — message us for today's stock.
How do I verify a car's registration?
Check the registration book and file, and verify the number on the Excise & Taxation online portal. We do this for you on every deal.

Have another question?

Ask us directly on WhatsApp — we are happy to help.
